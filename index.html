Mark C. Navin

PhD, BSN, RN, HEC-C

Philosopher, bioethicist, healthcare ethics consultant, and nurse. I work at the intersection of moral philosophy, public health, and clinical practice.

I work to bring philosophical inquiry to the practice of medicine, nursing, and public health. In addition to being a scholar, I am also a practicing healthcare ethics consultant. That work includes case consultation, ethics education, and policy development. I have led or served on institutional review boards, animal care and use committees, scarce-resource allocation committees, vaccine mandate exemption committees, and a hospital committee on therapeutic termination of pregnancy. More recently I completed a nursing degree, passed the NCLEX, and worked as a bedside nurse. I want to understand healthcare ethics from both inside and outside the system.

My research focuses on vaccine ethics and policy, clinical ethics, and public health ethics. I have published two monographs, Values and Vaccine Refusal (Routledge, 2016) and America's New Vaccine Wars (Oxford University Press, 2023, with Katie Attwell), along with three edited volumes and nearly a hundred articles and book chapters. My work appears in journals including JAMA, JAMA Pediatrics, Pediatrics, Milbank Quarterly, Hastings Center Report, and the American Journal of Bioethics.

My op-eds and essays have appeared in The New York Times, Time, STAT, NBCNews, and The Conversation. I have been interviewed by the Washington Post, Vox, NPR, CBC Radio, and outlets in Japan, Hong Kong, and Australia.

Topics

Vaccines and Immunization

  • Vaccine mandates and exemption policy
  • Vaccine hesitancy, refusal, and the people behind it
  • Vaccine communication with skeptical patients and parents

Pediatric Ethics

  • Pediatric assent and the role of children's preferences
  • When clinicians should override family decisions
  • Conflict between parents, between parents and clinicians, and between parents and adolescents

Clinical Ethics

  • Treatment over patient objection
  • Surrogate decision-making for incapacitated adults
  • Conscientious objection by clinicians
  • Informed consent at the bedside
  • The work of clinical ethics consultation

Public Health Ethics

  • The political philosophy of public health
  • Reciprocity, vulnerability, and obligations to the community
  • Scarce resource allocation (lessons from COVID and beyond)
  • Nudges, mandates, and the ethics of behavior change in public health

Nursing and Bedside Care

  • The moral labor of nurses in informed consent
  • Philosophy at the bedside (a philosopher's nursing education)
  • Hierarchy, delegation, and moral responsibility on clinical teams

Bigger Picture

  • The political-philosophy preconditions for ethical practice
  • Reflective equilibrium and clinical wisdom
  • How heterodox positions get marginalized in bioethics
